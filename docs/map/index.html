How to read it

  • Districts are shaded by unemployed people per 1,000 residents (all ages, so lower than the official rate, which counts only the labour force).
  • Dots are the 79 cities with 100,000+ residents, sized by population. Green ones are ranked; the darker, the more entry-level listings per 1,000 unemployed. White dots are cities that make up less than half of their district, where the district's unemployment says little about the city itself.
  • The top 5 rank first on a weighted mix of all three kinds of work (entry-level counts double). The bars show listings per 1,000 unemployed per kind of work; the black tick marks Berlin's value.
